Questling is now on Google Play 🎲

A diary of everything you play – video games, board games, cards, tabletop RPGs, phone games – set in a small tavern at the crossroads of worlds. Every session you write down feeds the creatures by its hearth, and the way you play decides who comes next.

• Sessions with a running clock or written down afterwards: the result, the score, the moment, a note, the screenshots
• A catalogue that fills in covers, years and genres; phone games found by themselves
• Dice, «who goes first», a turn timer and a score sheet for the table
• 36 pixel-art creatures, nearly 300 trophies, weekly quests and a season – nothing sold for money

Free, no ads, no account. Everything stays on your phone.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.questling
GitHub: https://github.com/bigdevwhale/questling

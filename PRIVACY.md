# Privacy

Questling has no account, no analytics and no ads. Your games, sessions, players, notes, pictures, creatures and trophies stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on; a backup file you save yourself goes wherever you put it.

Two things go over the network:

- **The game catalogue.** When you press «Look it up», the title you typed is sent to Wikidata and Wikipedia to find the game's year, maker, genres and cover. Nothing else goes with it, and nothing is sent unless you search.
- **The weather for the tavern window.** The coordinates of your town (guessed once from your time zone) are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached.

Two permissions are optional and off until you turn them on:

- **Photos (screenshots of a session).** If you let the app pick up a session's screenshots, it looks through the phone's pictures for screenshots made between the start and the end of that session, and copies the ones you tick into its own storage. It reads nothing else and sends nothing anywhere. Without this permission you can still add pictures through the system photo picker.
- **Usage access (phone games).** If you turn on «Find sessions on the phone», the app asks Android which game apps were on screen and for how long. The list never leaves the phone, and a found stretch becomes a session only when you confirm it.

**Photos of the table** are taken with your phone's own camera app and kept in Questling's private storage.

Share cards and pictures leave the phone only when you share them yourself.
